SIFOBOOKS LAN - THREE POS STATIONS

SERVER PC
- Run start-sifobooks-network.bat.
- Keep the central data\\sifobooks.db only on this server.
- Give the server a fixed LAN IP, for example 192.168.1.100.
- Allow TCP port 3000 through Windows Firewall on the private LAN.

POS PC 1 / 2 / 3
- Connect to http://SERVER-IP:3000.
- Use unique station codes POS-01, POS-02 and POS-03.
- Assign each station to its cashier/user account and role.
- Do not run an independent company database on the POS PC in LAN mode.

ROLES
- Company Administrator: company setup, users, stations, accounting, inventory, ZRA and reports.
- Manager/Supervisor: operational management and approved reports.
- Cashier: POS/till operations and own shift records only.

ZRA SMART INVOICE / VSDC
Each fiscal station must have its applicable ZRA identity recorded against the station. Store:
- ZRA branch code
- ZRA device ID where issued
- SDC ID where applicable
- device serial number
- VSDC endpoint
- ZRA environment (test/production)

Do not invent or reuse ZRA device identifiers. Enter the identifiers supplied/registered by ZRA. Fiscal transaction records must retain the station identity and the ZRA response data (receipt number, internal data, signature and QR information) returned by the approved VSDC integration.

The VSDC integration remains subject to ZRA approval/certification and the applicable ZRA Smart Invoice specifications.
